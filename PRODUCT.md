# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of trade and local service businesses in New Zealand and the UK: glaziers, balustrade and fencing installers, shower-enclosure companies and similar. They are good at their trade, not web people, and are busy. Most open the site on a phone, often from a link Matt has sent them, and are deciding whether to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

The portfolio and sales site for Sunzal Digital, Matt's one-person web design studio in Auckland. It shows who Matt is, what he does and the sites he has built, so a potential customer emails him. Success is an enquiry to matt@sunzaldigital.com.

## Positioning

One person does the work from the first chat to launch and beyond: no agency layers, no jargon, no surprise invoices. Matt knows the trades and builds sites around getting the client's phone ringing, including tools beyond a brochure site (an online shower designer, quote forms, a private CRM).

## Operating Context

- The link is sent to prospects directly, so visitors arrive cold and often on mobile.
- The only action is email; there is no form, phone number or booking tool on the site.
- Clients choose between Matt hosting and maintaining the site, or a Webflow build they can edit themselves.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: custom website design, mobile-first builds, Google and local search setup, quote forms and enquiries, online tools and portals (price calculators, design tools, CRM portals), Webflow builds the client edits, or hosting and maintenance by Matt.
- The site is static HTML and CSS in `public/`, deployed to GitHub Pages at sunzaldigital.com.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Sunzal Digital. Contact: matt@sunzaldigital.com. Matt is based in Auckland and works with businesses in NZ and the UK.
- Voice: first person ("I"), plain, friendly and direct, as Matt talks to a client on site.
- Logo, colours and type are documented in `public/brand/` and the guide at `/brand/`.

## Evidence on Hand

- Three live client sites, with screenshots in `public/images/`:
  - Balustrading Concepts, Auckland (balustrading.co.nz): gallery, a page per service, quote requests.
  - Brennan Glass, London (brennanglass.com): service and area pages, built on Webflow so the team can edit it.
  - Custom Showers, London (customshowers.uk): online shower designer that sends measurements and a panel schedule with each enquiry (it does not show a price), and a private CRM for quotes, jobs and invoices.
- None of the following exist, so none may be invented: testimonials, client logos beyond these three, prices, performance or ranking metrics, or a photo of Matt.

## Product Principles

1. Show the real work instead of making claims about results.
2. One clear action, email Matt, reachable from every part of the page.
3. Plain language over industry jargon.
4. Honest about scale: one person, three clients so far, two countries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Visitors are often on phones, outdoors or between jobs, so readable type, strong contrast and generous tap targets matter.
