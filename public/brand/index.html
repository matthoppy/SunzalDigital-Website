Brand guide

Sunzal Digital brand

The logo, colours, type and tone of voice that make Sunzal Digital look and sound like itself. Use these for the website, quotes, invoices, social profiles and email signatures.

Logo

The mark is a sun setting on the horizon, with its reflection in the water below. Simple enough to read at favicon size, and it gives the brand its warm accent colour. The wordmark is set in Geist and outlined, so it looks the same everywhere.

Give it room

Keep clear space around the logo at least the height of the sun on every side.

Keep it legible

Don't use the full logo smaller than 24px tall on screen or 8mm in print. Below that, use the mark on its own.

Don't redraw it

No stretching, recolouring, outlines, shadows or swapping the font. Use the files above as they are.

Colour

Mostly ink and paper, with sun used sparingly: in the logo, for the main call to action and for small accents. Sun should never be the background of a whole section.

Ink#0F1C26

Headings, body text, dark sections and buttons.

Paper#F5F2EC

Main background. Warm, not stark white.

Sun#C4461A

Logo, main call-to-action buttons (with white text) and accents.

Sun light#F08A4B

The sun on dark backgrounds.

Slate#4B5663

Secondary text and "digital" in the logo.

Stone#E4DED3

Dividing lines, borders and quiet panels.

Type

One family does almost everything. Geist is free from Google Fonts, so it works in Google Docs, Canva and on any computer.

Geist Aa

Headings in SemiBold (600) with tight letter-spacing. Body text in Regular (400), and Medium (500) for buttons and emphasis.

GEIST MONO

Small uppercase labels only, such as section labels, locations and captions. Never for paragraphs.

Voice

Write like Matt talks to a client on site: plain, friendly and direct. First person ("I"), not a faceless "we".

Sounds like us

  • "I'll get back to you with some ideas and a clear price."
  • "Most of your customers find you on their phone."
  • "No agency layers, no jargon, no surprise invoices."

Doesn't

  • "We leverage cutting-edge digital solutions."
  • "Unlock your brand's full potential."
  • Made-up statistics, or promises about rankings and results.
